# Account security and identity verification

## Why we verify your identity
Before discussing an order or making changes, our assistant asks for three details: your account email, an order number, and the 6-digit delivery PIN code of that order. This protects your orders and personal data from other people.

## What we will never ask for
Acme Outdoors will never ask for your password, OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, full card number, Aadhaar or PAN in chat, on calls or on social media.

## Failed verification
After several unsuccessful attempts, the chat is handed to a human specialist, who may verify you through your registered phone number.

## Orders from someone else's account
We can only discuss orders with the account holder. If you are helping a friend or family member, ask them to contact us directly.
