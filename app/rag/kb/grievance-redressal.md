# Grievance redressal and escalation

## Talking to a human
You can ask Acme Assist for a human specialist at any time. Complex cases, disputes, high-value refunds and legal matters are always handled by our specialist team.

## Grievance officer
In line with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, Acme Outdoors has a Grievance Officer. Email grievance@acmeoutdoors.example. We acknowledge every grievance within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within one month.

## National Consumer Helpline
If you are not satisfied, you can also contact the National Consumer Helpline (NCH) at 1915 or through the INGRAM portal. We would still love the chance to fix things first.
