# Order marked delivered but not received

## First steps
Sometimes parcels are handed to a neighbour, security guard or reception. Please check with them and wait 24 hours, as couriers occasionally mark an order delivered a little early.

## Raising a non-delivery claim
If you still have not received it, contact support within 7 days of the delivery date. We open an investigation with the courier partner, who reviews the proof of delivery (delivery OTP, signature, photo or GPS location). Investigations take 3-5 business days.

## Outcome
If the courier cannot prove delivery, we send a replacement or a full refund. If the proof of delivery confirms the parcel was delivered to your address (for example, with your delivery OTP), we will share the proof with you. Claims are reviewed by a specialist, not decided in chat.
