# GST invoices

## Getting your invoice
A GST tax invoice is emailed when your order is dispatched and can be downloaded from "My Orders". All prices on Acme Outdoors include GST.

## Business purchases (GSTIN)
To claim input tax credit, add your company name and GSTIN at checkout before placing the order. A GSTIN cannot be added or changed after the invoice has been generated, because invoices are reported to the GST portal.

## GST rates
Most apparel and footwear attract 12% GST (or 5% below the applicable price slab); equipment such as tents, backpacks and electronics attracts 18% GST. The exact rate and HSN code are printed on your invoice.

## Credit notes for returns
When a return is refunded, a GST credit note is issued automatically and emailed to you.
