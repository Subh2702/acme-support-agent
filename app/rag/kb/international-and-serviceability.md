# International shipping and serviceability

## Do you ship outside India?
No. At the moment Acme Outdoors delivers only within India. International cards are accepted for orders delivered to Indian addresses.

## Remote locations
We deliver to many remote trekking hubs such as Leh, Manali, Gangtok, Joshimath and Munnar. Delivery can take 7-12 business days, and cash on delivery may be unavailable in some high-altitude areas.

## APO and military addresses
Orders to APO/FPO addresses are delivered through India Post Speed Post and may take longer.
