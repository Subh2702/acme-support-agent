# Bulk and corporate orders

## Who qualifies
Trekking clubs, schools, adventure companies and corporate teams ordering 10 or more units of a product can request bulk pricing.

## How to order
Email corporate@acmeoutdoors.example with the products, quantities, delivery city and your GSTIN. Our team replies with a quote within 2 business days. Bulk orders are prepaid by bank transfer or card, and a GST invoice is issued in your company's name.
