# Cash on delivery (COD)

## Availability
Cash on delivery is available for orders up to ₹15,000 at most serviceable PIN codes. A COD handling fee of ₹49 applies. You can pay the delivery agent in cash or by scanning their UPI QR code.

## Why COD may be unavailable
COD may be switched off for very high-value orders, for PIN codes where our courier partners do not collect cash, or for accounts with repeated refused COD deliveries.

## Refunds on COD orders
COD refunds are made by bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS). A specialist will email or SMS you a secure link to enter your bank account and IFSC details; the transfer is made within 7 business days after the details are verified. For your safety, never share bank details in chat.
