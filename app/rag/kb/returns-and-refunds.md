# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The 30 days are counted from the delivery date shown in your order tracking. Items must be unused, with original tags and packaging. Socks and innerwear cannot be returned once opened, for hygiene reasons.

## How to request a return or refund
Contact Acme Assist (chat) or email support@acmeoutdoors.example with your order number. After a quick identity check, we will book a free reverse pickup from your delivery address. Our courier partner collects the item within 2-4 business days, and the refund is started once the item passes a quality check at our Bengaluru warehouse. Some refunds are reviewed by our refunds team before they are issued; you will get a ticket number and an update within 24-48 hours.

## Refund timelines by payment method
Refunds always go back to the original payment method:
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM): usually 2-3 business days.
- Credit and debit cards: 5-7 business days, depending on your bank.
- Net banking: 5-7 business days.
- Cash on delivery: paid by bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) within 7 business days after you submit your bank details through the secure link we email you.

## Cash-on-delivery refunds
Because COD orders were paid in cash, we refund them by bank transfer. A support specialist sends a secure link to your registered email or phone to collect your bank account details. Never share bank account numbers, card numbers, UPI PIN or OTP in chat, on calls or on social media; Acme staff will never ask for them.

## Partial refunds and one refund per order
You can return some items from a multi-item order and receive a partial refund for those items. Each order can be refunded only once, so please return everything you want to send back together.

## Late returns
Returns requested more than 30 days after delivery are not accepted. If an item developed a manufacturing defect, check our warranty policy instead.
