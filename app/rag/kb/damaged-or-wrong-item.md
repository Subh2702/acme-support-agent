# Damaged, defective or wrong item

## Report within 48 hours
If your parcel arrives damaged, an item is defective, or you received the wrong item or size, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos (an unboxing video helps a lot).

## What happens next
We arrange a free reverse pickup and send a replacement once the item is picked up. If a replacement is not in stock, you get a full refund to the original payment method.

## Missing items
If an item is missing from a multi-item order, check whether it is shipping separately (you will have a second AWB number). Otherwise report it within 48 hours and we will investigate with the courier.
