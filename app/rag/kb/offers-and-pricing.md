# Offers, coupons and pricing

## Coupons
Only one coupon can be applied per order. Coupons cannot be applied after an order is placed and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Price changes
Prices can change during sales. We do not offer price matching or refunds of the difference if the price drops after you order, but you may cancel a processing order and re-order at the new price.

## Prices include GST
All prices shown include GST. Shipping and COD charges, where applicable, are shown separately at checkout.
