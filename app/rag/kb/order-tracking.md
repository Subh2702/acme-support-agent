# Tracking your order

## Order statuses
- Processing: we have your order and are packing it. You can still cancel or change the address.
- Shipped: handed to the courier; track it with the AWB number.
- Delivered: the courier has marked it delivered.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled before shipping.
- Refunded: a refund has been issued for the order.

## Tracking link
Your AWB number and courier name are in the shipping SMS and email and in "My Orders". Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch.

## Delays
Weather in hill regions, strikes, and festive-season volumes can add 2-3 days. If your order is more than 3 days past the expected date, contact support.
