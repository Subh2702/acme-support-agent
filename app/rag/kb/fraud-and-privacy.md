# Fraud awareness and privacy

## Beware of fake calls and messages
Fraudsters sometimes pose as courier or support staff and ask for an OTP or a "refund processing fee", or send a UPI "collect request". Acme never charges a fee to process a refund, and you never need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Report suspicious calls to the National Cyber Crime helpline at 1930.

## How we use your data
We use your name, address and phone number only to process orders, deliveries and support requests, in line with our privacy policy and applicable Indian law. Chat transcripts are stored securely, and payment details are handled by PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways; we never see your full card number.
