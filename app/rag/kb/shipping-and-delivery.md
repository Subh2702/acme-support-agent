# Shipping and delivery

## Dispatch time
Orders are packed and dispatched within 1-2 business days from our Bengaluru warehouse. Orders placed after 2 PM IST or on Sundays and national holidays are processed the next business day.

## Delivery time
- Metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune): 2-4 business days after dispatch.
- Other cities and towns: 4-7 business days.
- North-East states, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep: 7-12 business days.

## Courier partners
We ship with Delhivery, Blue Dart, Ekart, DTDC, Xpressbees and India Post Speed Post, choosing the best partner for your PIN code. Your AWB (tracking) number is sent by SMS and email when the order ships.

## Shipping charges
Shipping is free on orders of ₹999 and above. Orders below ₹999 have a flat shipping charge of ₹79. Cash-on-delivery orders carry an additional COD handling fee of ₹49.

## Serviceable PIN codes
We deliver to over 19,000 PIN codes across India. Enter your 6-digit PIN code on the product page to check delivery and COD availability.
