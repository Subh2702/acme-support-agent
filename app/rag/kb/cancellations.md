# Cancelling an order

## When can I cancel?
You can cancel an order any time before it ships, while its status is "processing". Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled; you can refuse the delivery or request a return after it is delivered.

## Refunds for cancelled orders
For prepaid orders, the full amount is reversed to your original payment method as soon as the cancellation is confirmed. UPI reversals usually reflect within 2-3 business days; card and net-banking reversals take 5-7 business days. Cash-on-delivery orders are cancelled without any charge because nothing was paid.

## Partial cancellation
Individual items in a multi-item order cannot be cancelled separately today. Cancel the whole order and place a new one, or return the unwanted item after delivery.

## Did Acme cancel my order?
We occasionally cancel orders when an item goes out of stock, the PIN code is not serviceable, or a payment looks suspicious. You will get an SMS and email, and any payment is reversed automatically.
