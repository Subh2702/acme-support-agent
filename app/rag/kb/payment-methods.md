# Payment methods

## Accepted methods
- UPI: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM and any UPI app.
- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay and American Express.
- Net banking from all major Indian banks.
- Cash on delivery (see the COD article).

## EMI
No-cost EMI for 3 or 6 months is available on credit cards from major banks for orders above ₹3,000. The EMI conversion is done by your bank; interest refunds on cancelled EMI orders follow your bank's timelines.

## Payment deducted but order not placed
If money was debited but you did not receive an order confirmation, the amount is automatically reversed by the payment gateway within 5-7 business days. There is no need to pay again for the same order.

## International cards
International cards are accepted, but we only deliver within India.
