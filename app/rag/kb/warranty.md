# Warranty

## Warranty periods
- Tents, backpacks and sleeping bags: 1 year against manufacturing defects (seams, zips, poles).
- Trekking shoes and boots: 6 months against sole separation and stitching defects.
- Electronics such as headlamps and GPS watches: 1 year manufacturer warranty.
- Jackets and apparel: 6 months against manufacturing defects.

## What is not covered
Normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, accidents, fire, improper washing or unauthorised repairs.

## How to make a warranty claim
Contact support with your order number, a description of the defect and clear photos. After review, we will repair, replace or, if neither is possible, refund the item. Warranty claims are handled by our specialist team and usually take 7-10 business days.
