# Product care

## Tents
Dry your tent fully before packing to prevent mildew. Clean with lukewarm water and a mild soap; never machine wash or use detergent on the waterproof fly.

## Down jackets and sleeping bags
Wash rarely, on a gentle cycle with a down-specific wash, and tumble dry on low with dryer balls to restore loft. Store loosely, not compressed.

## Trekking shoes
Remove the insoles and air-dry after every trek. Do not dry leather boots near a heater or in direct sun, as the leather can crack. Re-apply waterproofing wax every few months.
